# The sidebar's words for every sharing choice, by setting key: the title of a group's switch, or
# a field's label and the line that says what it sends. SharingFields reads them; Settings and the
# step after Connect draw them, and SharingGroupsTest holds each label to RuneLite's own list.
# {FEED_KEPT} is MapCompanionConfig.FEED_KEPT.
sharePosition|Position and world|Your tile, world and instance type. Shows your dot where its location is known.
shareActivity|Training and idle time|The skill last trained, recent experience and time since moving.
shareAccountType|Account type|Your ironman or other account badge.
shareSkills|Skill levels|Your levels, for usable routes.
shareQuests|Travel quest completion|Progress in the quests that unlock travel; no individual steps.
shareQuestLog|Full quest progress|Started or finished states for the full quest and miniquest log; no individual steps.
shareDiaries|Achievement diaries|Completed tiers and collected rewards; no individual tasks.
sharePermanentTravel|Permanent travel and house|Verified permanent travel unlocks and the town your house is in.
shareCapabilities|Carried travel items|Derived yes/no travel flags; your inventory is never sent.
shareStash|STASH construction|Built STASH units; stored equipment is never read.
shareBoats|Boats and moorings|Where each boat is moored and its teleport focus, so voyages start at your boat; boat names are never sent.
shareSeaCharts|Sea charts charted|Which Sailing sea charting tasks you have charted.
shareSlayer|Slayer task|Your current assignment and remaining count, or what Mortimer offers while you choose.
shareClue|Clue step|Your current clue scroll step.
shareFarming|Farming patches|Patch states you have observed, for planning runs.
shareBirdhouses|Birdhouses|States observed by RuneLite Time Tracking, with their age.
shareHunterRumours|Hunter rumours|The rumour observed after checking a quetzal whistle; recheck after login or hopping.
shareMahoganyHomes|Mahogany Homes|The customer and tier observed in dialogue or game messages.
shareFarmingContracts|Farming contracts|The crop requested by your current Farming Guild contract.
sharePortTasks|Port tasks|Your Sailing port task slots and their counters; cargo is never read.
shareDailies|Dailies collected|Which of the dailies the map plans, like Zaff's battlestaves, you have collected today.
shareLoot|Observed kills and drops|Locally observed tracked monster kills and rated drop totals on normal members worlds; not lifetime kill counts.
shareMilestones|Notable drops and levels|Notable observed rewards and real levels, and quests and diary tiers finished while you play, if those are shared. Activity rewards require Loot Tracker. {FEED_KEPT}
sharePets|Include pet rewards|Observed pet rewards while Notable drops and levels is enabled; unknown pets stay unnamed.
shareCollectionLog|Include collection log additions|New collection notifications while Notable drops and levels is enabled; existing slots are never read.
shareName|Character name|The display name of the character you are logged in as.
shareDeath|Where you died|The tile of your most recent death, forgotten after an hour.
showDestination|Destinations and meetups|Receive destinations and meetup details. Nothing is clicked or walked for you.
showGroupOnWorldMap|Group on the world map|Receive the positions your group already shares and draw them on the world map.
showBoatsOnWorldMap|Your boats on the world map|Pin each of your boats at the port where it is moored. Nothing leaves this computer.
destinationHintArrow|Hint arrow to a destination|Point the game's own arrow at a destination once it is nearby. Nothing leaves this computer.
showStepOnScreen|Step on screen|While you follow a route, the step you are on in a small box over the game. Nothing leaves this computer.
outlineStepTeleport|Step's teleport outlined|While you follow a route, the item or spell a teleport step uses, outlined where you click it. Nothing leaves this computer.
drawWithShortestPath|Draw paths with Shortest Path|When you follow a route, the Shortest Path plugin draws each walking step. Nothing leaves this computer.
routeHereOnWorldMap|Route here on the world map|Right-click the world map for the way to that spot, or for a meetup there. A press sends that spot and where you stand, as Route does.
dropLuckChat|Drop luck in chat|One chat line when a drop with a known rate lands. Nothing leaves this computer.
markFarmRunInBank|Farm run teleports in the bank|Outline in the bank the teleports a farm run needs that you are not carrying. Nothing leaves this computer.
shareMapPresence|Map presence|
shareProgressAndTravel|Progress and travel|
shareTasksAndTimers|Tasks and timers|
shareLootAndMilestones|Loot and milestones|
notifyMeetups|Meetup reminder|A RuneLite notification before a meetup starts. Ticked here, it comes while you play too; how it arrives follows RuneLite's own notification settings.
