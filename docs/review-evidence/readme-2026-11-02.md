# README pictures — 2 November 2026

The UI review of the sidebar landed as twelve pull requests, and each left the README's pictures
alone so they could be drawn once, after every surface had settled. These are those ten plugin
pictures. The two website pictures, `website-slayer.jpg` and `website-sailing.jpg`, are unchanged:
the review was of the plugin. `readme-2026-10-29.md` is kept as the record of the pictures these
replace.

## How they were made

Each is the real Swing component painted at the sidebar's 225px, 2× into a 450px-wide PNG, with
RuneLite 1.13.4's look and feel, as before. `sidebar-polish-2026-10-29/SidebarPolishShot.java`
paints nine; `sailing-voyage-2026-11-01/VoyageShot.java` paints the voyage. From the plugin root,
after `./gradlew harnessJar` (Windows needs the classpath entries as Windows paths):

```sh
cp=$(tr -d '\r\n' < build/harness/classpath.txt)
javac -proc:none -encoding UTF-8 --release 11 -cp "$cp" -d build/readme-shot-classes \
  docs/review-evidence/sidebar-polish-2026-10-29/*.java docs/review-evidence/sailing-voyage-2026-11-01/*.java
export OSRS_MAP_PUBLIC=../osrs-stars-map/public
run="java -Djava.awt.headless=true -Duser.timezone=Australia/Sydney \
  -Dlogback.configurationFile=docs/review-evidence/sharing-sidebar-2026-10-11/logback.xml -cp build/readme-shot-classes:$cp"
$run com.pbolduc.mapcompanion.SidebarPolishShot <out> route search-whip-card farm-count group stars-route \
  dropluck-card slayer-card settings-closed port-run
$run com.pbolduc.mapcompanion.VoyageShot <out>
```

The time zone only puts the meetups' clocks in an evening; a render prints whatever the clock says.
No request leaves the machine: the map address is a port nothing listens on.

## What the scenes needed to reach

- **Sharing as the one press leaves it.** A paired scene used to share position and skill levels
  alone, which the status card now names as two groups partly shared. Every paired scene shares
  `RecommendedSharing.KEYS`, so the card says *Sharing all but where you died* and Settings shows
  five switches on and *Where you died* off. *Group on the world map* stays off, so a friend's row
  still offers **World map**.
- **A settled session.** Every scene starts with a settled reading of no Slayer task, so Home's
  Slayer card says *No Slayer task* rather than the line it shows in the first seconds after a login.
- **`route`** stands at Draynor's bank, before the cast, so the step on now is the one with the spell's
  icon, and opens *1 other way* to show the ring of dueling's sprite leading it. Shortest Path is no
  longer installed in it: Follow is offered on every route.
- **`group`** adds a second meetup tomorrow, which the tab folds to one line, and keeps the stream
  fixture's own news for the third friend, a quest, which it used to rewrite as a level while the
  client announced no quests. The quest's mark is the world map's quest-start sprite, the website's
  copy of cache sprite 1454.
- **`stars-route`** is new: W302 at Rimmington mine picked, and Route's answer shown the way
  `RoutePress` shows one, through `RoutePress.destination`, `DestinationState.selectLocal` and
  `DestinationRoute.adoptPressed`: pinned, its steps under the star, and followed, so its first button
  says **Pause**. The way, `starRoute()`, is written for the picture: the Rimmington tablet's landing
  from the map's `data/transports.json` (`teleport_item:2953,3224,0`), the walk to the star fixture's
  site tile, and 29 tiles, the walk plus the tablet's four ticks at a run, as the map prices a tablet.
  No map planned it. The tablet's picture is the website's travel chip,
  `osrs-icons/1x/teleport-tab-rimmington.png`, which `Sprites.CHIPS` now reads for an item the website
  keeps no other copy of.
- **`farm-count`** is new. The run fixture's teleports, Explorer's ring 2 and the Catherby tablet, are
  none the client can see in a bag, so its closed checklist never counts. The scene adds a *Digsite
  pendant* line, the teleport a birdhouse run takes to Fossil Island, and carries nothing, so
  *Before you leave · 1 not on you* shows; and it stands at the Falador patch it has just replanted,
  so Catherby is next with *This leg* still ahead of it, rather than *You're here* over a leg not yet
  walked. `farm-bring` and `farm-card` are unchanged.

## Limits

- **No game ran.** RuneLite was not launched; every value is synthetic and every name made up.
  Sprites are the website's copies of the game's art, read through `OSRS_MAP_PUBLIC`, where a running
  client draws them from its own caches. The world switcher's two stars on Stars' P2P and F2P chips are
  the renderer's stand-ins, drawn in their colour and size, as in October.
- **Words in a second colour.** Painting a layout made at 1× at 2× opens a small gap before words
  drawn in another colour partway along a line: on Stars, before a time left and before
  *· Wilderness*. The same states painted at 1× have none. For that reason the Group scene does not
  file the player's world, so the star hunt's *W302* keeps the line's grey: at the end of that long
  line, the green a client draws while you are on the world opened a gap about as wide as *W302*.
- **Not yet seen in a live client**, as `docs/live-checks.md` lists: a friend's quest news and its
  mark, Before you leave's count, a star's route drawn under the star, and the steps' sprites.
