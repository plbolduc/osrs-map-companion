# README pictures — 13 November 2026

A review of the sidebar as released in 1.9.0 changed eight pictured surfaces: the ways to a
destination are a strip of chips over its steps, a search row and a farm stop say how far at the
right, a friend's row leads with their world, a star site's fold points the way every other fold
does, and a long charting task wraps beside its length. `docs/sidebar-layout.md` says what each is,
under *Ways as chips, and a number at the right*. These are the eight pictures painted again.
Every other picture is as `readme-2026-11-02.md`, `readme-2026-11-06.md` and
`tasks-cards-2026-11-11.md` record it.

## How they were made

As on 2 November: the real Swing component painted at the sidebar's 225px, 2× into a 450px-wide
PNG, with RuneLite 1.13.4's look and feel, by `sidebar-polish-2026-10-29/SidebarPolishShot.java`
and `sailing-voyage-2026-11-01/VoyageShot.java`. From the plugin root, after
`./gradlew harnessJar -PruneLiteVersion=1.13.4`, on Windows with JDK 25:

```sh
cp=$(tr -d '\r\n' < build/harness/classpath.txt)
javac -proc:none -encoding UTF-8 --release 11 -cp "$cp" -d build/readme-shot-classes \
  docs/review-evidence/sidebar-polish-2026-10-29/*.java docs/review-evidence/sailing-voyage-2026-11-01/*.java
export OSRS_MAP_PUBLIC=../osrs-stars-map/public
run="java -Djava.awt.headless=true -Duser.timezone=Asia/Bangkok \
  -Dchart.run=docs/review-evidence/chart-run-2026-11-12/kharidian-sea.json \
  -Dlogback.configurationFile=docs/review-evidence/sharing-sidebar-2026-10-11/logback.xml -cp build/readme-shot-classes;$cp"
$run com.pbolduc.mapcompanion.SidebarPolishShot <out> route search-whip-card farm-count group stars-route \
  dropluck-card slayer-card settings-closed port-run chart-run quest-route
$run com.pbolduc.mapcompanion.VoyageShot <out>
```

The time zone only puts the meetups' clocks in an evening at the hour it was painted, and
`-Dchart.run=` gives `chart-run` the kept run, as before. No request leaves the machine: the map
address is a port nothing listens on. Painted twice, the two sets were the same pixel for pixel.

## Limits

- **No game ran.** RuneLite was not launched; every value is synthetic and every name made up.
  Sprites are the website's copies of the game's art, or the three kept beside the renderer.
- **No picture shows a voyage among other ways.** The strip over a sail, with the boat's chip
  beside a teleport's, is drawn by the code and held by `RouteStepsViewTest`, and is in no
  picture here.
- **No world is green.** The Group scene does not file the player's world, for the gap a second
  colour opens at 2×, as `readme-2026-11-02.md` says, so Cara's *W302* keeps the line's grey; a
  client on W302 draws it green. The destination card's green world is in no picture.
- **Not yet seen in a live client**, as `docs/live-checks.md` lists: the chips and a press on
  one, a pick kept through Reroute, a search row's distance, a farm stop's length and the tiles
  left, a friend's world, and the fold's arrow.
