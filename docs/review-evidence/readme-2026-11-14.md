## How it was made

As on 13 November, with one correction: the README's scene is `dropluck-boss-card`, not
`dropluck-card`, the Aberrant spectre card it was before the Dagannoth Kings had rates
(`readme-2026-11-02.md` says so; the 13 November command line still named the old one). From the plugin
root, after `./gradlew harnessJar -PruneLiteVersion=1.13.4`, on Windows with JDK 25, with `cp` and
`run` set as that record says:

```sh
$run com.pbolduc.mapcompanion.SidebarPolishShot <out> dropluck-boss-card
```

The scene kills Nex 212 times alone, with the platelegs at kill 64, the horn at 120 and the
vambraces at 137 — the case `DropOddsTest` and the map's tests pin. Its item catalog is a stub that
knows only the names it shows, so it answers Nex's table by name as the real catalog does in the
game, and `Sprites.java` gives each of her items the website's copy of its picture; without them a
row not yet had paints with no picture, which the game would not do.

## Painted again at the sidebar's width

The first paint of this picture was 225px wide, the width of the whole sidebar. The card sits inside
the sidebar's 10px insets, so in the game it has 205px, and a review measured rows there that the
picture could not show: each drop's name and rate were wrapped at one width for every row, wider
than the room beside a *Normal* or *Very lucky* chip, and cut mid-letter past it. The rows now wrap
at what the picture and their own count leave, and `SidebarPolishShot` paints `dropluck-boss-card`
at 205px, 2× into a 410px PNG, with the same command as above. The README shows it 273px wide, the
scale its 450px pictures have at 300px, so its words are the size of theirs.

Nothing it says changed: in this font every row of the Nex card fitted at 225px and still fits at
205px, *1 in 43, +10% as MVP* beside the *Dry* chip included. Painted twice, the two were the same
pixel for pixel, and it was looked at for a cut word, an overlap or a missing sprite, and had none.

Only this scene was moved. The other section scenes — `search-whip-card`, `farm-count`,
`chart-run`, `slayer-card`, `port-run` and `quest-route`, and `VoyageShot`'s `voyage-sail` — still
paint at 225px, 20px wider than the sidebar gives them, until their pictures are painted again and
looked at. The scenes of the whole panel are right at 225px, its insets included.
