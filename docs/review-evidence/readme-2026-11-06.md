# README pictures — 6 November 2026

names friends as the second reason to install. Two website pictures were missing for that:
nothing showed friends on the map, and nothing showed a route that is more than one teleport and a
walk. These are those two. Every other picture is as `readme-2026-11-02.md` records it.

## `website-group.jpg`

`scripts/companion-story.mjs` runs the real plugin classes through the integration harness against
a dev server with a database of its own, so the group, its members' positions, Bob's quest and the
meetup are what the plugin sent and the map drew, not a mock. The story names its group *Story
clan* and its meetup *Story meetup*; for the picture a copy was run with those two strings replaced
by *Wednesday crew* and *Barrows run*, and with `TZ=America/Los_Angeles` so the meetup's clock
reads in an evening. Nothing else in the copy changed, and it passed all 97 of the story's checks,
as the original did on the same checkout. Bob and Cara are the story's own made-up characters.

## `website-route.jpg`

The `map-ynysdail` scene from `scripts/shoot-map.mjs`, which `docs/ui-review.md` in the map's
repository describes: the card's fastest way to Ynysdail with Route pressed, five steps from the
Sailors' amulet through two ships, and the four numbered pins on the map. The route is the
planner's answer, not one written for the picture.

The `map-chained-boarding` scene, the Chambers of Xeric bank, was tried first and photographed
nothing on this checkout; it was not used and not investigated here.
